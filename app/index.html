            AI first pass (Gemini or Claude)

            The AI model reads each section of the plan for room names, numbers and likely takeoff rooms. Only the plan images are sent: to Google for Gemini models, to Anthropic for Claude models. Use paid keys: Google’s free tier may use content to improve its products.

            The app tells the two apart. Keys are saved on this computer only (config/anthropic_key.txt for Claude, config/gemini_key.txt for Gemini). Never paste a key into a chat or email.

            Cost per sheet is roughly 12 sections × ~1,500 tokens in + ~400 out for Gemini, ~6,000 in + ~1,500 out for Claude (it reads the image at higher resolution).

            Use your key’s limit: for Gemini, AI Studio (Usage → rate limits), where 15 is the free-tier limit; for Claude, platform.claude.com (Settings → Rate limits): requests per minute for the model you use (4,000 on the Start tier; 300 is plenty).
